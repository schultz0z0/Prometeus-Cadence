06How it behaves

Business rules observed in the product, each with its evidence. They become the acceptance tests of the new product.

Offered rules (not backend proofs) 18 rules

R-001 A trial is distinct from preparation

Given an inactive trial, when the account is opened, then preparation is offered and sending requests trial activation. [obs EV-003]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-002 Catalog has explicit cycle

Given monthly or annual selection, when the catalog changes, then total charge and monthly equivalent are distinguished. [obs EV-004] [obs EV-005]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-003 Plan and purchased enrichment balances differ

Given monthly and purchased credits, when consumption occurs, then UI describes plan credits first and perpetual purchased balance second; plan resets day 1. [obs EV-027]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-004 Invoice contact is not login identity

Given a billing contact, when edited, then its purpose is billing and it need not match login. [obs EV-007] [obs EV-023]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-005 Enrichment only charges useful results

Given eligible qualified companies with CNPJ, when enriched, then preview is free and no result is described as no credit spent. [obs EV-027]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-006 Enrichment has distinct cache windows

Given previously resolved or unresolved companies, when reconsidered, then UI describes 6-month positive reuse and 45-day negative cooldown. [obs EV-027]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-007 First cadence is a reviewed draft

Given AI generation, when options are shown, then user chooses and edits before draft creation; generating alone does not create the cadence. [obs EV-061]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-008 Campaign and accepted cadence are separate

Given a LinkedIn invitation, when accepted, then subsequent messaging belongs to a cadence and lead conversion follows enrichment. [obs EV-044] [obs EV-045] [obs EV-067]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-009 Public comments need approval

Given AI-written public comments, when ready, then they wait for human approval before publication. [obs EV-049]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-010 SDR is Pro only

Given a non-Pro account, when SDR is opened, then an upgrade gate describes 300 conversations and economy/premium weights. [obs EV-043]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-011 Retention removes text but keeps trace

Given configured retention, when bodies expire, then metadata remains and AI should not use removed text; minimum inbound/outbound periods differ. [obs EV-016]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-012 Webhook destinations are bounded

Given outgoing events, when configured, then up to 10 destinations and selected list/event filters are offered. [obs EV-074]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-013 Webhook secret signs content

Given a secret, when a POST is delivered, then UI documents X-Webhook-Signature with HMAC SHA-256 and an idempotency key. [obs EV-074]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-014 Owner and MCP members differ

Given a member without MCP permission, when connecting, then access must be controlled separately from the owner. [obs EV-013]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-015 Inbox distinguishes application read state

Given a received email, when listed, then unread/favorite/bounce and lead-only filters are independent offered dimensions. [obs EV-041]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-016 Cadence pause/archive/delete differ

Given a cadence, when archived, then UI says pause and hide without deletion; activation and deletion are separate actions. [obs EV-058]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-017 Dispatch estimates are not delivery promises

Given a LinkedIn daily target, when queued, then UI says weekly limits and account capacity may lower the result. [obs EV-067]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

R-018 Report period is explicit

Given report filters, when switching period/cadence, then overview, funnel, CRM and performance use the chosen scope. [obs EV-040] [obs EV-055] [obs EV-056] [obs EV-057]

Not exercised transactionally in L4. Acceptance must probe real outcomes in owned fixtures.

10Build plan

What to build and in what order, the acceptance tests, and what to copy, avoid and improve.

Intent: reproduce observed product structure, workflows and capabilities under Prometeus branding, retaining explicit user-approved differences: managed OpenWA replaces Twilio; conservative OpenWA limits; customer-configurable AI; monthly subscription plus prepaid consumption packs. No competitor account/backend is an implementation dependency.

  6. 07A: LinkedIn complete, independent workflows. R-008, R-009, R-017.

Definition of done

An area closes only after its UI, server contracts, data persistence, permissions, background execution, failure/reconciliation and evidence-based acceptance are complete. Previous implementation milestone is not retrospectively called nonexistent; its remaining parity work is tracked separately. All references are in clone/parity.md.

Not included automatically

Reference's promotional response rates, proprietary model text/assets, third-party subscription giveaways, mandatory bespoke setup fees, legal wording and provider-ban guarantees. They are not behavior to fabricate in our product. [obs EV-060] [obs EV-080] [doc SRC-002]
