# Production barriers

## BR-001 Reference contract and ownership

Category: contract/IP. Features: literal copying of code/assets/text/identity. Gatekeeper: reference owner. Severity: blocks that copying approach. Market weight: high for a direct clone. Lead time/cost: unknown. Section 8 restricts copying and reverse engineering. User decision belongs to the user; record without accepting new terms. Mitigation: independent implementation from observed behavior, own brand and original templates. [doc SRC-002]

## BR-002 Unavailable connected and paid evidence

Category: access/integration. Features: operational SDR, connected LinkedIn/CRM/Sheets, sends, invoices. Severity: blocks parity proof, not all local implementation. Market weight: high. Lead time/cost: depends on user-owned test accounts. Mitigation: fixture providers plus later real authorized sandbox pilot; do not start a paid trial or send to real people in the reference. [obs EV-043] [obs EV-053] [obs EV-065]

## BR-003 Provider and data rights

Category: integration/commercial. Features: enrichment data, LinkedIn automation, hosted AI. Severity: may block feature rollout. Gatekeeper: chosen providers. Market weight: high. Lead time/cost unknown pending contract. Mitigation: licensed data adapter and confirmed hosted provider rights; no inference that public marketing grants usage rights. [obs EV-054] [obs EV-067] [inf] Q-011

## BR-004 Real financial activation

Category: payments. Features: production subscriptions and invoices. Severity: blocks live commerce until merchant account/configuration and test acceptance exist. Market weight high. Cost depends on own Stripe account; not estimated here. Mitigation: sandbox first, hosted checkout, signed lifecycle events, own catalog and ledger. [doc SRC-004] [doc SRC-005]

## BR-005 OpenWA availability and number risk

Category: platform dependency. Features: own-number WhatsApp. Severity: adds operational risk. Gatekeeper: WhatsApp platform and self-hosted gateway. Mitigation: dedicated number, conservative rate, explicit risk notice, reconnection/reconciliation and official alternative. No daily cap guarantees avoiding restrictions. [obs EV-082]
